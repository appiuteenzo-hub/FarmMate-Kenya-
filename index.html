🌾 Crops
Crop information for Kenya

Crop Guides

🌽 Maize

Use quality seed and plant when suitable moisture is available. Maintain proper spacing, control weeds and manage soil fertility. Monitor regularly for pests and diseases.

🫘 Beans

Use healthy seed and well-drained soil. Control weeds early and monitor the crop for pests and diseases.

🥔 Irish Potatoes

Use certified clean planting material where available. Prepare loose, well-drained soil and avoid waterlogging.

🍅 Tomatoes

Use healthy seedlings, maintain adequate moisture and monitor regularly for pests and diseases. Support plants where appropriate.

🥬 Vegetables

Select varieties suitable for your area and target market. Maintain soil fertility, moisture and regular pest monitoring.

🐄 Livestock
Livestock management information

Livestock

🐄 Dairy Cattle

Provide clean water, balanced feed, suitable housing and regular health monitoring. Keep reliable farm records.

🐐 Goats

Provide adequate feed, clean water and shelter. Monitor animals regularly and seek veterinary assistance when animals show signs of illness.

🐔 Poultry

Maintain clean housing, suitable feed, clean water and appropriate disease prevention practices.

🐑 Sheep

Provide suitable grazing or feed, clean water and shelter. Regularly monitor body condition and general health.

🌦️ Weather
Weather planning

Weather

🌧️ Rainfall

Rainfall affects planting, crop growth, harvesting and soil moisture. Avoid unnecessary field operations during heavy rainfall.


☀️ Dry Conditions

During dry periods, consider moisture conservation practices such as mulching and appropriate irrigation where available.


🌡️ Weather Planning

Before spraying, planting or harvesting, check a reliable weather forecast for your location.
