🌾 Crop Guide

Maize
Needs well-drained fertile soil, adequate moisture and good weed management.
Beans
Plant in fertile, well-drained soil. Avoid waterlogging and control pests early.
Tomatoes
Require good sunlight, fertile soil and regular but controlled watering.
Irish Potatoes
Prefer cool conditions and well-drained fertile soils.
Vegetables
Use organic matter, adequate water and proper pest management.

🌦️ Weather

Allow location access to check your current weather.

🐄 Livestock Guide

Cattle
Provide clean water, balanced feed, shelter and regular disease control.
Goats
Provide browse, clean water, shelter and routine parasite control.
Sheep
Maintain good nutrition, hygiene and disease prevention.
Poultry
Provide clean water, proper feed, ventilation and biosecurity.
Pigs
Provide balanced feed, clean housing and good disease management.

🧪 Soil Guide

Soil pH
pH affects nutrient availability and crop growth. Many crops perform well around slightly acidic to neutral conditions.
Soil Fertility
Maintain fertility using organic manure, appropriate fertilizers and good crop rotation.
Soil Moisture
Avoid both severe drying and prolonged waterlogging.
Organic Matter
Compost and well-decomposed manure can improve soil structure and nutrient supply.

🐛 Pests & Diseases

Fall Armyworm
Scout maize regularly and use integrated pest management.
Aphids
Monitor young leaves and shoots. Encourage beneficial insects and use appropriate control measures when necessary.
Fungal Diseases
Use healthy planting material, proper spacing and good field sanitation.
Integrated Pest Management
Combine monitoring, cultural, biological and appropriate chemical control methods.
