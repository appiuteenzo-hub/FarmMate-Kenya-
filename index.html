🌾 Crop Guide

🌽 Maize

Use quality certified seed and plant when adequate rainfall begins. Weed early and monitor pests.

Major Kenyan crop

🫘 Beans

Use healthy seed in well-drained soil. Control weeds and monitor diseases and insects.

Short-season crop

🍅 Tomatoes

Use good seedlings, provide support and maintain consistent moisture. Monitor pests and diseases.

High-value crop

🥔 Irish Potatoes

Use certified disease-free seed, prepared soil and good drainage.

🥬 Vegetables

Examples include kale, spinach, cabbage and traditional leafy vegetables.

Check rainfall and soil moisture before planting. Protect crops during extreme weather and avoid unnecessary irrigation after heavy rainfall.

🐄 Livestock Guide

🐄 Cattle

Provide clean water, adequate feed, shelter and routine disease prevention.

🐐 Goats

Provide quality forage, clean water, shelter and regular health monitoring.

🐑 Sheep

Maintain good nutrition and hygiene and monitor for parasites and diseases.

🐔 Poultry

Provide balanced feed, clean water, proper ventilation and biosecurity.

🐖 Pigs

Keep housing clean and dry and provide balanced feed and clean drinking water.

🧪 Soil Guide

pH

Soil pH shows how acidic or alkaline soil is. Many crops perform well in slightly acidic to neutral soils, although the ideal range depends on the crop.

🌱 Soil Fertility

Important nutrients include nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium. Organic matter can improve soil structure and nutrient availability.

💧 Soil Moisture

Good soil should retain enough water for plants while allowing excess water to drain.

♻️ Organic Matter

Compost and well-decomposed manure can improve soil structure, water retention and biological activity.

🐛 Pests & Diseases

🐛 Fall Armyworm

Commonly affects maize. Monitor young plants and inspect leaves regularly for feeding damage.

🍃 Aphids

Small insects that suck plant sap. Monitor new growth and use appropriate integrated pest management practices.

🍂 Fungal Diseases

Symptoms may include spots, rotting or wilting. Improve field sanitation and avoid prolonged leaf wetness where possible.

🛡️ Integrated Pest Management

Use resistant varieties, field sanitation, monitoring, biological control and pesticides responsibly when necessary.
